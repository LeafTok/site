Changelog

Track our progress and see what's new in each version of LeafTok.

v3.0.3

August 2026
  • FixedFixed a crash that could stop the app from opening. The AI narration voice was loading on every launch — even for readers who never used it — and now loads only when an AI voice is chosen.

v3.0.2

August 2026
  • FixedFixed a memory leak when opening several books in a row
  • ImprovedFaster swiping in long books — no more slowdown as you get deeper in
  • ImprovedBetter reporting of low-memory shutdowns so we can catch them sooner

v3.0.1

July 2026
  • FixedFixed crashes while navigating chapters and favoriting cards
  • FixedFixed a crash during narration auto-advance
  • ImprovedImage-heavy books use far less memory and no longer get shut down by iOS

v3.0.0

July 2026
  • NewBook Clubs — create a club, invite friends with a share code, and read together
  • NewChapter discussions inside the reader, with spoiler flags and emoji reactions
  • NewReader profiles with a display name and emoji avatar
  • ImprovedInvite codes are no longer case-sensitive

v2.1.0

May 2026
  • FixedFixed crashes when backgrounding the app while audio or narration was playing
  • FixedFixed a crash when closing the reader as narration finished
  • FixedLarge books no longer get the app shut down for using too much memory
  • FixedThe app recovers gracefully instead of crashing if local data is corrupted
  • FixedThe sleep timer can now be cancelled mid-countdown

v2.0.0

May 2026
  • NewRebuilt from the ground up as a native iOS app — faster and smoother throughout
  • NewKokoro AI voices for natural-sounding narration
  • NewAmbient sound generator — white, brown and pink noise, rain, forest and cafe
  • ImprovedNew local storage engine for quicker library loading

v1.9.4

December 2025
  • ImprovedStability improvements

v1.9.3

November 2025
  • NewFont size controls in the reader — four sizes with live preview
  • ImprovedYour font size choice is remembered between sessions
  • ImprovedMusic controls are off by default for a cleaner reading screen
  • FixedFixed PDF text extraction on Android

v1.9.0

October 2025
  • NewPDF processing now works fully offline — no network required
  • ImprovedPDFs load faster, with support for files up to 100MB
  • ImprovedClearer explanation of what can be pulled out of a PDF
  • FixedFailed PDFs now explain themselves instead of showing a network error

v1.8.2

October 2025
  • NewEPUB table of contents with nested chapter navigation
  • NewLandmark and page-number navigation for reference books
  • ImprovedRicher book details — contributors, series and subjects
  • ImprovedBetter handling of tables, figures, footnotes and equations
  • FixedFixed audio player stability and library layout issues

v1.7.0

January 2025
  • NewReading statistics — time read, cards read and books finished
  • NewReading streaks with daily goals
  • NewShare cards to Instagram Stories, WhatsApp and Twitter
  • ImprovedFaster card loading and smoother animations

v1.6.0

  • NewTap anywhere to show or hide the reader controls

  • FixedFixed missing images in books imported with older versions
